# Data attribution and separation

Source: Dingdong-Inc, **FreshRetailNet-LT**, version 1.0, released 2026-02-02. [Official release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dingdong-Inc/FreshRetailNet-LT). [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The release contains normalized daily/hourly sales and hourly stockout annotations. Quantities are not prices, currency or recoverable physical units. Raw files remain outside this repository; acquisition verifies their locked hashes.

Changes: deterministic identity subset; selected-field projection; derived features, forecasts, tables, figures and fitted models. The 56-record fixture retains source facts but adds explicitly **synthetic** arrivals. Capacity, costs and injected faults are project assumptions. Dataset-derived material retains this attribution and CC BY 4.0 notice; no source endorsement is implied. Original software and narrative are separately MIT-licensed.

The release's technical-report/arXiv entry is a placeholder and is not cited as a published paper. FreshRetailNet-50K is a related but different dataset and is not substituted for this LT release.
