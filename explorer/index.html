What makes the time boundary defensible?

Fit ends 2024-12-31; validation selects the point model in January–February; interval calibration uses March–April; final scoring begins May 1. The external July 14–20 week is a short additional check. Future realized weather, discounts, activity and stock status are excluded. Model identity binds configuration and serving implementation, and each job freezes both its event and monitoring context.

How much certainty do the intervals support?

What happens to disappearing series?

The original cohort is preserved. Only 656 of 1,024 selected identities appear in the external week. Errors condition on observed, usable rows; neither missing days nor disappeared products are fabricated as zero sales. Held-out identities have historical lags, so this is transfer with history, not cold start.

02 / Decision sensitivity

Optimal for the stated grid.
Useful only under stated assumptions.

Eight items per final date. A 0.1 normalized-quantity grid, assumed capacity, and equal-weight raw-quantile / point scenarios.

Mean realized proxy cost across 74 dates. These values are not currency.
RuleProxy costShortage proxyExcess proxy

The allocator solves its finite scenario objective exactly; it does not establish a real-world optimum. Observed sales are only a demand proxy. Equal scenario weights are assumptions, and the middle point is a mean forecast, not an estimated median.
Why include an oracle and a losing case?

The hindsight grid oracle uses future observed sales and is unattainable prospectively. It is a same-grid lower bound, not an implementable competitor. The scenario rule loses to proportional allocation at the 1 : 1 cost ratio. That sensitivity is part of the result.

Decisions with a durable record.

A single-machine application with tested failure boundaries. No request-time model fitting and no cloud-production claim.

Features, forecasts and result publish atomically. Stale workers cannot overwrite a result.

Actual loopback HTTP

200 measured jobs after 20 warm-ups; four clients; one worker; repeated 28-day real fixture. Shared local host; the receipt identifies the environment. Unavailable OS memory telemetry is reported as unmeasured.

04 / Limits are part of the result

What this work does not establish.

Organic demand recovery

Hidden-hour reconstruction uses artificially masked, fully in-stock observations. It does not identify real lost sales.

Population or production performance

History eligibility, attrition, shared hardware and a small warm workload restrict generalization.

Operational savings

No actual inventory, costs, lead times or physical capacity are available. Simulated quantities and costs are explicit assumptions.
